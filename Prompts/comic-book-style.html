AI VFX Prompts for a Comic Book Style

Comic book style results depend heavily on which era and tradition you reference. Silver Age superhero printing is distinct from gritty 80s inked comics, which is distinct from modern clean graphic novels. Name the era for a coherent result.

Key prompt elements

  • Era — Silver Age, 80s gritty inked, modern graphic novel, manga
  • Outline weight — heavy uniform outlines, variable ink weight, crosshatching
  • Colour treatment — flat four-colour, limited palette, full vibrant
  • Halftone — where dots appear and their size
  • Paper texture — aged newsprint, clean white, yellowed pulp

Example prompts

  • Silver Age comic book style: bold black ink outlines, flat four-colour printing aesthetic, CMYK halftone dots in shadow areas, slightly misregistered colour fills for period authenticity, aged newsprint texture.
  • Modern graphic novel: precise variable-weight outlines, clean flat colour fills with cel-shaded shadows, no halftone, high contrast black and deep colour palette, smooth white page surface.
  • Manga ink: thin precise outlines, black and white with screentone dot patterns, simplified backgrounds, expressive character rendering, clean white manga paper. No colour fills.
