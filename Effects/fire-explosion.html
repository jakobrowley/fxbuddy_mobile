AI Fire and Explosion VFX in Premiere Pro

Add fire, smoke, and explosions to any clip from inside your timeline. FXbuddy generates pyrotechnic effects that integrate with your scene's lighting and perspective — no stock overlay, no separate compositing session.

How it works

  1. Select a clip from your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline.
  2. Describe the pyrotechnic effect in FXbuddy — size, position, type (campfire, building fire, mid-air explosion), and how it should move.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to its AI engine, which generates the fire integrated into the footage. Returns in 60–120 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Add a large explosion in the background right of frame at the 1-second mark. Fireball with rolling black smoke.
  • Add a contained campfire in the foreground left. Warm flickering orange light.
  • Make the building in the background look like it is on fire. Flames in the upper windows.
  • Add a car explosion in the middle distance. Fast initial fireball, then rolling black smoke.
