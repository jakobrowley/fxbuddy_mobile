AI VFX Prompts for Dust and Debris

Dust and debris prompts need to describe the trigger (impact, wind, explosion), particle size and density, movement direction, and how the particles catch available light. Backlit dust reads very differently from dust in flat light.

Key prompt elements

  • Trigger — impact, explosion, wind, vehicle passing
  • Particle characteristics — fine dust, coarse gravel, wood splinters, concrete chunks
  • Movement — rising cloud, horizontal scatter, falling
  • Light interaction — backlit particles, shadowed dust in diffuse light
  • Scale — personal-space debris versus room-filling cloud

Example prompts

  • Fine dust cloud rising from a footstep on dry earth, backlit by low sun, golden particles dispersing slowly upward and left in a slight breeze.
  • Explosion debris — concrete chunks, plaster dust, and shattered glass scattering outward from mid-frame, heavy particles falling fast, fine dust billowing up and back.
  • Sand and soil kicked up by a vehicle passing at speed, rooster tail of brown dust behind and to the right, thick cloud close to the ground thinning toward the top.
