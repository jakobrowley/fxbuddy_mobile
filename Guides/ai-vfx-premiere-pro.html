AI VFX in Premiere Pro: Complete 2026 Guide

FXbuddy is a Premiere Pro plugin that lets you add AI VFX to any clip from a text prompt — scene relighting, sky replacement, object removal, rotoscoping, fire, style transfer, and more. No compositing background required.

What you can generate

  • Scene relighting — change the lighting quality and direction of any clip
  • Sky replacement — swap overcast skies for dramatic alternatives
  • Object removal — erase crew, rigs, logos, and unwanted elements
  • Background replacement — new environments without a green screen
  • AI rotoscoping — isolate subjects without frame-by-frame masking
  • Fire and explosion VFX — grounded effects that match your footage
  • Style transfer — apply cinematic looks, art styles, and film aesthetics
  • Weather and atmosphere — add rain, fog, snow, and mist

How it works

  • Select a clip in your timeline
  • Open the FXbuddy panel (Window → Extensions → FXbuddy)
  • Choose the effect type and write your prompt
  • Generate — the clip processes in the cloud
  • Apply — the result drops back onto your timeline
