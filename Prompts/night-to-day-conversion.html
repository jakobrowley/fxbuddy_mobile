AI VFX Prompts for Night to Day Conversion

Night-to-day conversion is one of the most technically demanding time-of-day changes because the AI needs to infer what hidden shadow detail would look like in daylight, reconstruct the sky, and shut off all practical lighting. Strong prompts describe the target day in detail.

Key prompt elements

  • Target daytime — overcast, sunny midday, morning light, afternoon
  • Sky type to replace the night sky
  • Whether artificial lights (street lights, windows) should turn off
  • Shadow quality of the new daylight
  • Ambient light temperature shift — from cool night to warm daylight

Example prompts

  • Convert this night exterior to a sunny midday scene — replace night sky with bright blue overcast sky, turn off street lights and lit windows, add neutral daylight shadows from above, warm the overall colour temperature significantly.
  • Convert night to an overcast grey day — flat diffuse daylight, no visible sun, muted colours, turn off all artificial lights, bring up exposure uniformly while retaining scene detail.
  • Convert night to golden morning — low sun from camera left, warm amber light, soft directional shadows, sky orange-to-blue, street lights off, windows unlit, dew visible on surfaces.
