AI Weather and Atmosphere Effects in Premiere Pro

Add rain, snow, fog, mist, haze, and atmospheric conditions to any clip from inside your timeline. FXbuddy generates weather that integrates with your scene's perspective and lighting — not a generic overlay slapped on top.

How it works

  1. Select an outdoor or window-visible clip on your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline.
  2. Describe the weather or atmospheric condition — intensity, type, and any specific characteristics like wind direction or mist density.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to Runway Gen-4 Turbo, which generates the weather integrated into your scene. Returns in 60–90 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Add heavy rain to this street scene. Wet pavement reflections, rain streaks visible in the light.
  • Add thick early-morning fog rolling in from the left. Distant trees barely visible.
  • Make it snow. Steady medium snowfall. White accumulation visible on car roofs and pavement.
  • Add a low-lying ground mist. Misty valley at dawn. Mist sits at knee height, clear above.

Best for

  • Establishing shots needing emotional weather to support narrative mood
  • Continuity fixes when angles were shot on different weather days
  • Seasonal flexibility — summer footage that needs to read as winter
  • Documentary B-roll from the wrong season
