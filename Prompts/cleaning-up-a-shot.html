AI VFX Prompts for Cleaning Up a Shot

Shot cleanup prompts are most effective when you list every element to remove in a single clear request. Describing each problem element with its frame position and movement helps FXbuddy prioritise and handle the full cleanup in one pass.

Key prompt elements

  • Enumerate all elements to remove with their approximate frame position
  • Describe whether each element is static or moving
  • Describe the background content behind each element to reconstruct
  • Whether any subjects in the shot should remain untouched
  • Priority if the shot is complex — which elements matter most

Example prompts

  • Clean up this shot: remove the safety cable running from the subject's back to upper-right corner, the boom mic visible at top-center for the first 3 seconds, and the crew member visible in the lower-left background. Reconstruct the sky, ceiling, and wall behind each. Primary subject untouched.
  • Clean this interview shot: remove the power strip on the floor bottom-left, the water bottle on the table behind the subject, and the visible C-stand leg entering from the right edge. Keep the subject and interview framing intact.
