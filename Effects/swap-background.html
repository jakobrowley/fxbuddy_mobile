AI Background Swap in Premiere Pro

Replace the entire background of any clip with an AI-generated environment — no green screen required. FXbuddy isolates the subject and generates a new background in one pass.

How it works

  1. Select a clip on your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline. Works best on shots with the subject clearly separated from the background.
  2. Describe the new background in FXbuddy — environment type, time of day, lighting, and atmosphere.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to Runway Gen-4, which handles subject isolation and background generation in one pass. Result returns in 60–90 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Replace the background with a modern Tokyo street at night. Neon signs, rain-wet pavement.
  • Swap the background for a clean minimalist white studio. Soft wraparound lighting.
  • Change the background to a dramatic mountain vista at sunset.
  • Replace with a cozy coffee shop interior. Warm tungsten light, slightly out of focus.

Best for

  • Corporate and talking-head videos recorded in imperfect locations
  • Lifestyle and social content where the shooting location wasn't ideal
  • Product demos placed in different environments from the same clip
  • Music videos iterating on background environments
