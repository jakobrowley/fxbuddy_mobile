AI VFX Prompts for Glow and Light Rays

Glow, bloom, and volumetric light ray effects anchor to existing light sources in your shot. Describe which light source to enhance, the direction of rays, and the colour temperature of the glow to get results that feel physically connected to your scene rather than pasted on top.

Key prompt elements

  • Light source — sun position, practical lights, through a window or doorway
  • Ray type — volumetric god rays through atmosphere, lens flare streaks, aura glow
  • Direction and spread — radiating outward from source, angled through particles
  • Colour temperature — warm golden sun, cool blue dawn, neutral white practical
  • Intensity — subtle haze versus dramatic beam

Example prompts

  • Volumetric god rays from a window upper-left, warm golden light streaming through dusty air, rays angled at 45 degrees, soft and atmospheric, not overpowering the subject.
  • Aura glow radiating from a glowing object in the subject's hand — soft white-to-gold bloom that spills onto the subject's face and chest, gentle pulsing quality.
  • Lens flare as sun moves behind subject — classic film lens flare with anamorphic horizontal streak, warm amber, appearing as sun crests the edge of the subject's shoulder.
