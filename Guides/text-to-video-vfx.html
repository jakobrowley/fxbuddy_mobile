Text-to-Video VFX: How AI Generates Effects from Prompts

Text-to-video VFX lets you describe a visual effect in plain language and have the AI generate it integrated into your existing footage — no compositing tools or specialist skills required. FXbuddy brings this directly into Premiere Pro and After Effects.

What makes a good prompt

  • A specific effect description: not "make it cinematic" but "warm golden-hour light from camera right"
  • A style or quality qualifier: "soft diffused light," "film grain," "hard-edged practical shadow"
  • A preservation instruction: what not to change — "preserve the foreground subject's face"
  • Placement detail for added elements: "fire rising from behind the subject's left shoulder"

Where AI VFX excels

  • Environmental and atmospheric effects: sky replacement, weather, fog, rain, snow
  • Scene relighting: changing light direction, quality, and temperature
  • Object removal: boom mics, rigs, power lines, logos
  • Style transfer: cinematic looks, film aesthetics, art styles
  • Speed: most 5-second clips generate in under 2 minutes

Current limitations

  • Dialogue lip-sync: AI VFX does not alter or generate speaking faces
  • Photorealistic close-up face work for hero shots
  • Precise motion graphics, lower-thirds, and branded assets
  • Frame-perfect motion vector VFX for broadcast
