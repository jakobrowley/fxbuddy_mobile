AI VFX Prompts for Anime Style Transfer

Anime style transfer converts live-action footage into illustrated animation. The key is specifying which anime sub-style you're targeting — classic cel-shaded looks behave very differently from watercolour or modern digital flat styles.

Key prompt elements

  • Anime sub-style — classic cel-shaded, Studio Ghibli watercolour, 90s hand-drawn, modern flat digital
  • Outline weight — bold black outlines, thin precise lines, no outlines
  • Colour palette — saturated, muted pastels, limited palette
  • Background treatment — stylised same as subject, or kept realistic
  • Facial treatment — whether to simplify or maintain naturalistic features

Example prompts

  • Convert to classic cel-shaded anime: bold black outlines, flat colour fills with two-tone shading, saturated palette, simplified anime facial features. Maintain character identity and expression throughout.
  • Apply a Studio Ghibli watercolour style — soft painterly backgrounds with delicate texture, warm earthy palette, gentle linework, and naturalistic character rendering with anime proportions.
  • Apply anime watercolour style to background only. Keep the foreground subject realistic and unaltered.
