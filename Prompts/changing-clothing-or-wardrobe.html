AI VFX Prompts for Changing Clothing or Wardrobe

Wardrobe change prompts work best when the replacement is plausible given the original clothing's shape and movement. Changing a colour is more reliable than changing a silhouette. For structural changes, describe the new garment type, fabric behaviour, and fit relative to the original.

Key prompt elements

  • Original garment description so FXbuddy knows what to change
  • New colour and material — cotton, leather, silk, knit
  • Whether to change the garment type or only the colour/pattern
  • Fabric behaviour — stiff vs. draping, wrinkle pattern
  • Whether accessories should change as well

Example prompts

  • Change the subject's white T-shirt to a deep navy blue. Keep the same cut and fit. Maintain fabric texture and wrinkle behaviour from the original throughout the clip.
  • Replace the subject's casual jeans and T-shirt with a fitted black business suit — jacket and trousers. Keep the same body proportions and movement. Fabric should read as tailored wool.
  • Change the subject's plain grey hoodie to a red leather jacket. Keep the same general silhouette. Leather should reflect the scene's ambient light appropriately.
