AI VFX Prompts for Changing Time of Day

Time-of-day conversion requires describing both the new time and how that time's light behaves physically — sun position, sky colour, shadow direction, and ambient light colour. The more accurate your description of the target lighting environment, the more convincing the result.

Key prompt elements

  • Target time — golden hour, midday, dusk, twilight, night
  • Sky description — cloud coverage, colour at horizon vs. zenith
  • Sun or moon position and direction of shadows
  • Ambient light colour temperature
  • Whether artificial lights should switch on (street lights, windows)

Example prompts

  • Convert this midday scene to late golden hour — sun low on camera right, warm orange-amber light, long shadows falling left, sky orange near horizon transitioning to pale blue above, slight lens flare.
  • Change to a blue-hour twilight — sun below horizon, sky deep navy to orange near the horizon, street lights switched on and visible, cool blue ambient light on the ground, no harsh shadows.
  • Convert to night — deep dark sky, moon not visible, street light pools of warm amber on the ground, building windows lit from inside, cool blue ambient from the open sky.
