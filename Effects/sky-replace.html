AI Sky Replacement in Premiere Pro

Swap the sky in any outdoor shot — grey overcast to golden sunset, midday haze to dramatic storm clouds — from inside your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline. FXbuddy's AI pipeline generates a temporally consistent sky replacement across the full clip.

How it works

  1. Select an outdoor clip with visible sky on your timeline.
  2. Describe the sky you want in FXbuddy — time of day, cloud type, colour temperature, and any atmospheric conditions.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to its AI engine, which returns a new version with the sky replaced in 60–90 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Replace the grey overcast sky with a warm golden-hour sunset. Orange and pink tones on the horizon.
  • Swap the sky for dramatic dark storm clouds. Approaching thunderstorm.
  • Change to a clear deep-blue sky with scattered white cumulus clouds.
  • Replace with a night sky. Stars visible. Slight blue-purple ambient light.
