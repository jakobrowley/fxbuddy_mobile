AI VFX Prompts for a Golden Hour Look

Golden hour is the 30–60 minutes after sunrise and before sunset — a specific physical lighting condition, not just a warm filter. Describing the sun position, shadow direction, sky gradient, and atmospheric haze gives FXbuddy what it needs to generate a convincing result on your clip.

Key prompt elements

  • Sun position relative to frame — camera left, right, behind subject
  • Shadow length and direction — long shadows away from sun source
  • Sky gradient — orange-amber at horizon transitioning to pale blue above
  • Atmospheric haze — warm dust or moisture in the air catching the light
  • Rim light — whether the sun creates a warm edge on subject's hair/shoulders

Example prompts

  • Apply golden hour lighting: low sun from camera right, warm amber directional light, long shadows falling left, golden rim light on the subject's right shoulder and hair, orange sky visible in the background, slight atmospheric haze.
  • Backlit golden hour — sun directly behind subject, strong warm rim light around the silhouette, lens flare as sun peeks around the subject, sky orange and pink, slight overexposure on background.
  • Golden hour landscape — low warm light raking across the foreground terrain, long shadows in all horizontal details, sky golden-to-blue gradient, warm ambient fill light on shadow sides of objects.
