AI VFX Prompts for Removing People from Shots

Removing people requires more context than removing static objects — people move, cast shadows, and occlude the background. Specify their position in the frame, how they're moving, and the background content that should be revealed after removal.

Key prompt elements

  • Number of people and their frame position
  • Movement — walking, standing, crossing frame
  • Background to reconstruct — street, wall, open field, crowd
  • Whether to remove their shadow as well
  • Whether they overlap with the primary subject

Example prompts

  • Remove the two pedestrians walking across the background from left to right. Reconstruct the street and building facade behind them. Do not alter the primary subject in the foreground.
  • Remove the crew member standing in the upper-left corner throughout the clip. Replace with the continuation of the brick wall visible to their right.
  • Remove all background pedestrians — approximately five people at varying distances. Reconstruct the park path and lawn behind each of them. Keep the foreground couple untouched.
