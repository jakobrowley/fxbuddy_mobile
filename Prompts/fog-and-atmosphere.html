AI VFX Prompts for Fog and Atmosphere

Fog and atmosphere effects add depth, mystery, and environmental character to any scene. The key distinction is whether fog is ground-hugging, mid-air, or full-frame — and whether it moves and how it interacts with light sources in the scene.

Key prompt elements

  • Fog height and density — ground-level wisps, mid-frame bands, full-frame blanket
  • Movement — slow drift, static, rolling in from one side
  • Light interaction — fog catching shafts of light, backlit glowing haze
  • Colour temperature — neutral white, cool blue morning mist, warm amber evening haze
  • Depth layering — whether near fog is denser than far fog

Example prompts

  • Low ground fog rolling slowly across a forest floor — dense at ground level, thinning above knee height, cool blue-white colour, slight movement from right to left, backlit softly from behind the trees.
  • Thick grey overcast atmosphere — diffuse haze reducing visibility to mid-distance, no sharp horizon, muted desaturated colours throughout, flat ambient light with no directional shadow.
  • Atmospheric haze in a dimly lit interior — cigarette smoke-quality drift near ceiling level, warm amber tint from practical light sources, visible shafts of light cutting through the haze.
