AI VFX Prompts for Swapping Vehicles

Vehicle swap prompts need to describe the replacement vehicle in enough detail for FXbuddy to match the original's size, position, orientation, and movement in the shot. Staying close to the original vehicle's scale and general silhouette produces the most convincing result.

Key prompt elements

  • Original vehicle type and approximate size in frame
  • Replacement vehicle — type, era, make/model if specific
  • Colour and finish of the replacement
  • Whether the vehicle is in motion and at what speed
  • Background and ground interaction — shadows, reflections

Example prompts

  • Replace the modern sedan driving through frame with a 1969 Ford Mustang fastback, same deep red colour, matching the same speed and trajectory. Keep the background and road unchanged.
  • Replace the delivery van parked in the background with a vintage 1970s VW camper van in cream white. Same parked position and orientation. Background unchanged.
  • Replace the police car in the pursuit shot with a black tactical SUV. Keep the same speed, direction, and flashing light glow. Match the vehicle's size in frame.
