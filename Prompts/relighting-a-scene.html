AI VFX Prompts for Relighting a Scene

Scene relighting goes beyond colour grading — it physically re-simulates where light is coming from. The best prompts describe the light source, its direction, its colour temperature, how hard or soft it is, and what shadow quality it should produce.

Key prompt elements

  • New light source type — sun, window, candle, neon sign, overhead fluorescent
  • Direction — from camera left, from above, from behind the subject, from below
  • Hardness — hard direct sunlight, soft diffused window light, candle flicker
  • Colour temperature — warm amber, cool blue, mixed practical lights
  • Shadow quality — hard shadows, soft shadows, no visible shadow

Example prompts

  • Relight with warm golden-hour sun from camera left, hard directional light, long shadows falling to the right, warm amber fill, deep shadow side. Exterior afternoon look.
  • Change to moody blue neon from above and behind subject, cool rim light creating a blue edge on hair and shoulders, deep shadow in the face, urban night exterior.
  • Candlelit interior — warm amber flickering light from below frame center, soft and dynamic, casting upward shadow on the face, dark background with no other light source.
