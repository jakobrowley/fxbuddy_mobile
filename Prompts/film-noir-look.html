AI VFX Prompts for a Film Noir Look

Film noir is more than black-and-white — it's a specific combination of high contrast, hard directional shadow, and period grain that creates tension in every frame. Describing the shadow source and direction is essential for a convincing result.

Key prompt elements

  • Tonal range — crushed blacks, preserved midtones, clipped highlights
  • Shadow character — hard single-source directional, venetian blind stripes
  • Atmosphere — cigarette smoke, fog, rain haze
  • Grain — heavy period grain, fine grain
  • Selective colour — whether any element should stay in colour

Example prompts

  • Classic film noir: high-contrast black and white, crushed shadows, single hard light source from upper-left, strong shadow fall across the subject's face, heavy film grain, slightly overexposed background.
  • Venetian blind shadow noir: side-lit high contrast black and white, venetian blind shadow stripes across subject and background, medium grain, atmospheric cigarette smoke haze.
  • Neo-noir with selective colour: high-contrast desaturated monochrome except retain the neon sign in fully saturated red. Hard shadows, moody underexposure, fine grain.
