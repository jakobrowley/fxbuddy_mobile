AI VFX Prompts for Water and Rain

Water effects in FXbuddy require detail about rainfall intensity, angle, and how water interacts with surfaces in frame. Describing depth layers — foreground streaks versus background haze — separates convincing rain from flat overlays.

Key prompt elements

  • Rainfall intensity (light drizzle, heavy downpour, stormy) and angle
  • Wet surface reflections on pavement, windows, or vehicles
  • Foreground streaks vs. background atmospheric haze
  • Splash impacts on surfaces — puddles, ground, glass
  • Ambient moisture — breath visible, water running off surfaces

Example prompts

  • Heavy rain falling at a slight angle right to left, large droplet splashes on pavement, wet street reflections, foreground streaks sharp and fast, background reduced to grey haze.
  • Light evening drizzle — fine misty rain, wet cobblestones reflecting ambient light, soft atmospheric moisture, no hard foreground streaks.
  • Ocean spray from waves entering the lower frame, large water droplets in slow motion, backlit by sun, catching light as mist diffuses through air.
