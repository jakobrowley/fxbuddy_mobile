AI VFX Prompts for a Cyberpunk Neon Look

The cyberpunk neon look requires naming the dominant neon colours, specifying their direction as light sources, and describing wet surfaces for reflections. Generic prompts produce a flat dark grade — specific colour pairs produce convincing neon-lit results.

Key prompt elements

  • Dominant neon colour pair — magenta and cyan, blue and green, purple and gold
  • Light source direction — from above, side, rim, below
  • Shadow colour — cool blue-black, deep indigo, dark purple
  • Wet surfaces — pavement reflections, glass, chrome
  • Atmosphere — neon haze, rain mist, indoor smoke

Example prompts

  • Cyberpunk neon grade: magenta dominant right-side light, cyan fill from left, crushed shadows with cool blue-black tones, neon haze in air, heavy colour bloom on light sources, fine digital noise. Urban night aesthetic.
  • Night exterior: darken ambient to night level, overhead magenta and cyan neon, wet street reflections of neon colour in puddles, rain haze at mid-distance, shadows crushed to deep indigo-black.
  • Interior neon bar: electric blue from left, hot pink rim behind subject, deep purple shadow, heavy atmospheric fog, neon bleeding onto reflective surfaces, fine digital grain.
