AI VFX Prompts for Weather Changes

Weather change prompts need to cover both the sky and the ground — rain changes the pavement, snow changes the colour of everything, storm clouds change the ambient light. Describing how the environment reacts to the weather is as important as describing the weather itself.

Key prompt elements

  • Weather type — rain, snow, overcast, storm, fog, clear
  • Intensity — light drizzle, heavy downpour, blizzard, passing showers
  • Sky treatment — cloud type, coverage, colour
  • Ground-level changes — wet pavement, snow accumulation, puddles
  • Ambient light shift — flat overcast, dramatic pre-storm, cold winter white

Example prompts

  • Convert to a rainy day — overcast dark grey sky, heavy rainfall, wet streets with reflections, flat cold ambient light with no shadows, slight atmospheric haze in the background.
  • Add winter snowfall — overcast white-grey sky, light snow falling, existing surfaces dusted with snow accumulation, cold flat ambient light, pale and desaturated overall colour.
  • Convert to approaching storm — rapidly darkening sky with dark cumulonimbus from one side, contrast between sunny foreground and dark background, heightened wind implied in foliage, dramatic light.
