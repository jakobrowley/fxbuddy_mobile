AI VFX Prompts for Lightning and Electricity

Lightning and electricity effects require precise description of the arc path, colour, branching behaviour, and the light it casts on surrounding elements. Describing the strike source and the secondary illumination separates convincing electrical effects from simple overlays.

Key prompt elements

  • Strike type — cloud-to-ground, cloud-to-cloud, arc between objects, Tesla coil
  • Bolt colour and brightness — blue-white, orange-red, purple plasma
  • Branching and forking behaviour
  • Secondary illumination — blue-white flash on subject or environment
  • Duration and repeat — single strike, continuous arc, pulsing

Example prompts

  • Single powerful cloud-to-ground lightning strike from upper-right frame to lower-left, bright blue-white, multiple branches, brief intense flash illuminating the whole scene, rolling thunder implied.
  • Electrical arc between two metal objects in frame, continuous pulsing blue-purple electricity, crackling sparks at contact points, persistent glow around the arc.
  • Energy pulse radiating outward from subject's hands — electric blue tendrils branching outward in all directions, casting blue fill light on the subject's face.
