AI VFX in After Effects: Complete 2026 Guide

FXbuddy runs as a native After Effects panel, letting compositors and motion designers generate AI VFX on any footage layer from a text prompt. The generated clip returns as a new layer in your composition — no external application required.

Best uses for compositors

  • AI rotoscoping — clean subject mattes across entire clips without manual masking
  • Scene relighting — relight source footage to match a different background plate
  • Object removal — clean source clips before they enter your compositing stack
  • Style transfer — unify footage looks before compositing multiple sources
  • Background replacement — replace environments without green screen keying

How it fits into the AE layer workflow

  • Select a footage layer in your composition
  • Open FXbuddy panel (Window → Extensions → FXbuddy)
  • Choose effect type and write your prompt
  • Generated clip returns as a new layer above the original
  • Treat it like any other footage layer — apply masks, effects, expressions
