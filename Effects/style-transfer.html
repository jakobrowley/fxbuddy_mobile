AI Style Transfer in Premiere Pro

Transform any clip into a different visual aesthetic — film noir, anime, oil painting, Blade Runner neon, 16mm grain, 1970s Kodachrome — without leaving your timeline. FXbuddy uses its AI video engine to re-render your footage in a described style.

How it works

  1. Select a clip on your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline.
  2. Describe the target aesthetic in FXbuddy. Reference a film, an era, a medium, or a visual characteristic.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to its AI engine, which re-renders the footage in the described style. Returns in 60–120 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Make this look like it was shot on 16mm film in the 1970s. Kodachrome colour, visible grain.
  • Apply a noir black-and-white film look. High contrast shadows, single hard-key light.
  • Transform this into a hand-drawn anime style. Clean line art, flat shading, Studio Ghibli colour palette.
  • Make this look like a Blade Runner-style cyberpunk night scene.

Best for

  • Flashbacks and memory sequences with a specific film-era look
  • Music videos iterating on visual aesthetics per section
  • Title sequences transformed into stylized animations
  • Commercial content with a brand-appropriate aesthetic
