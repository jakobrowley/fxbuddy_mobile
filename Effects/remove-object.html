AI Object Removal in Premiere Pro

Erase microphones, logos, power lines, unwanted props, or distracting background elements from your footage without leaving your timeline. FXbuddy sends the clip to Runway Gen-4 and fills the gap with a plausible background.

How it works

  1. Select the clip and set in/out points around the section that needs cleaning up.
  2. Describe what should be removed and where it is in frame in FXbuddy's panel.
  3. Runway Gen-4 inpaints the removed area across the full clip duration. Result returns to your timeline as a new layer.

Example prompts

  • Remove the boom microphone from the top of the frame. Fill with the ceiling behind it.
  • Erase the coffee cup on the desk in the lower left. Replace with the table surface.
  • Remove the parked van from the right side of the street scene.
  • Clean up the power lines crossing the sky in the establishing shot.

Best for

  • Equipment mistakes (boom mics, C-stand legs, lighting rigs)
  • Uncleared logos or signage
  • Location distractions in documentary or run-and-gun footage
  • Product shot cleanup
