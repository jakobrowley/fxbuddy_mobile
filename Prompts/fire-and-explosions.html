AI VFX Prompts for Fire and Explosions

Fire and explosion VFX in FXbuddy work best when you describe the fire's source, scale, colour, and how it interacts with the surrounding scene. Generic prompts produce generic fire — specific prompts produce grounded results.

Key prompt elements

  • Fire source and position in frame (ground level, from a car engine, spreading from an object)
  • Fire scale — campfire versus massive explosion
  • Smoke colour, density, and direction
  • Background interaction — whether heat distortion or light cast should appear
  • Ember behaviour — rising, drifting left, heavy fall

Example prompts

  • Realistic campfire in the lower-left corner, warm orange and yellow flame, light smoke drifting upward, embers rising, casts warm fill light on nearby subjects.
  • Large controlled explosion mid-frame, expanding orange fireball with thick black smoke above, debris scattered outward, heat distortion visible in the air around it.
  • Car engine fire spreading from the hood, low rolling flames, white smoke with dark leading edge, no explosion — realistic slow burn.
