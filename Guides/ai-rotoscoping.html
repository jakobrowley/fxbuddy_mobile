AI Rotoscoping in 2026: Complete Guide

AI rotoscoping lets you isolate subjects from backgrounds across an entire video clip using a text prompt — no manual mask drawing, no frame-by-frame work. FXbuddy brings AI roto directly into Premiere Pro and After Effects.

How AI rotoscoping works

  • Describe the subject to isolate in a text prompt — position, clothing, relative depth
  • The AI tracks and isolates the subject across every frame simultaneously
  • The result drops onto your timeline as a new layer — original clip untouched
  • Use the isolated subject for background replacement, colour grading, or compositing

AI roto vs. Roto Brush 2 and Mocha

  • FXbuddy: text prompt only, no paint stroke required, works inside Premiere Pro
  • Roto Brush 2: requires manual paint stroke to initiate, After Effects only
  • Mocha Pro: most precise for complex shots, steep learning curve, expensive licence
  • Best for: FXbuddy for speed and Premiere-native workflow, traditional tools for hero VFX precision

What AI rotoscoping struggles with

  • Hair against similarly-coloured backgrounds — soft edges in these cases
  • Translucent elements: veils, frosted glass, gauze fabric
  • Fast motion blur obscuring subject edges
  • Water, glass, and reflective surfaces
  • Very long clips — split into shorter segments for more consistent results
