AI Rotoscoping in Premiere Pro

Isolate subjects from their backgrounds without drawing a single mask. FXbuddy uses Runway Gen-4 Aleph to automatically roto your clip, then drops the isolated subject back onto your timeline in under 90 seconds.

How it works

  1. Select the clip on your timeline and set in/out points for the section that needs rotoscoping.
  2. In FXbuddy, describe what you want isolated — "the person on the left," "the car in the foreground."
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to Runway Gen-4 Aleph, which generates the isolation and returns the result in 60–120 seconds.

Example prompts

  • Isolate the person on the left side of frame. Remove the background entirely.
  • Roto out the subject in the centre of frame. Replace the background with black.
  • Isolate the motorcycle in the foreground. Keep the rider attached.
  • Extract just the hands and product from frame.

Best for

  • Interview isolations for compositing onto new environments
  • Product lifts for marketing content
  • Motion graphics where text needs to flow behind the subject
  • Social media subject extraction for background-replacement effects
