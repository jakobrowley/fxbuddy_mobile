AI VFX Prompts for Rotoscoping a Subject

Rotoscoping prompts in FXbuddy work best when you describe the subject clearly, the edge treatment you need, and the intended use — a clean alpha matte for compositing, or a specific compositing result like placing the subject on a new background.

Key prompt elements

  • Subject description — human, animal, object, vehicle
  • Edge treatment — clean hard edge, soft feathered edge, hair/fur detail
  • Whether to include or exclude the subject's shadow
  • If replacing the background, describe it in the same prompt
  • Motion complexity — slow deliberate movement vs. fast action

Example prompts

  • Isolate the human subject from the background — clean matte with soft feathered edge, including hair detail. Place the isolated subject on a plain black background.
  • Rotoscope the subject and replace the background with a snowy mountain landscape at dusk. Subject should cast a soft shadow on the new background consistent with the new light direction.
  • Isolate the foreground car from the background street scene. Hard clean edge. Place on a white studio background for a product-style composite.
