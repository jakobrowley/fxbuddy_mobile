AI VFX Prompts for Erasing Cables and Rigs

Rig and cable removal prompts are most effective when you describe exactly what's visible, where it is in frame, how it moves, and what background should be revealed once it's gone. A wire that crosses the subject needs to be handled differently from one that runs against a plain sky.

Key prompt elements

  • What's being removed — safety wire, flying rig, support arm, boom mic, harness
  • Path through the frame — crossing the subject, behind the subject, against the sky
  • Motion — whether the wire/rig moves with the subject or is static
  • Background to reconstruct — sky, building, clean floor
  • Whether it crosses the primary subject or only appears in background

Example prompts

  • Remove the thin safety wire attached to the subject's back, running diagonally from their lower back to the upper-right corner of frame. Replace with the sky visible around it. The wire moves with the subject throughout.
  • Remove the wire flying rig visible as a horizontal bar across the upper third of frame. Replace with the sky and building behind it. Rig is static throughout the clip.
  • Remove the boom mic dipping in from the top of frame for approximately 2 seconds at the beginning of the clip. Replace with the ceiling texture visible around it.
