AI VFX Prompts for Background Replacement

Background replacement prompts need to describe both the new environment and the lighting that environment would cast on the foreground subject. A new background that doesn't affect the subject's light and shadow will look composited — describe the integration.

Key prompt elements

  • New environment type and setting — city street, forest, studio, mountain range
  • Time of day and weather in the new background
  • How the background light affects the foreground subject
  • Depth — whether the background has near, mid, and far elements
  • Movement — static background, parallax-appropriate motion, live environment

Example prompts

  • Replace background with a busy Tokyo street at night — neon signs, pedestrians, light rain on pavement. Background lighting should cast warm coloured light spill on the subject from the left. Keep foreground subject sharp, background slightly out of focus.
  • Replace background with a mountain summit at golden hour — sweeping peaks behind, warm orange sky, foreground subject lit by matching warm directional light from the right.
  • Replace background with a clean white photography studio cyclorama — soft even light from front, no visible seams, subject and background share the same flat white ambient light.
