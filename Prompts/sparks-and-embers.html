AI VFX Prompts for Sparks and Embers

Sparks and embers are high-energy, fast-moving particles that need precise description of their source, trajectory, and density to read as physically grounded. The difference between welding sparks and fire embers is in the velocity, size, and arc of the particles.

Key prompt elements

  • Source — welding arc, grinder, bonfire, pyrotechnic charge
  • Particle velocity — fast sharp sparks vs. slow drifting embers
  • Trajectory — radiating outward, falling downward, drifting upward
  • Density — sparse individual sparks vs. dense shower
  • Colour — orange-red embers, white-hot sparks, blue-white electrical sparks

Example prompts

  • Welding sparks showering downward from an off-screen source above frame, white-hot fast-moving particles, high density, short trails, falling in a cone shape and extinguishing on contact with ground.
  • Fire embers rising slowly from a bonfire at the bottom of frame, large glowing orange-red particles with long trails, drifting upward and slightly right, fading as they rise.
  • Angle grinder sparks — dense shower of fast white sparks fanning outward in a 60-degree arc from the lower-left corner, bright and short-lived, mixed with occasional larger debris.
