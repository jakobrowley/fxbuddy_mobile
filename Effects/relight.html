AI Scene Relighting in Premiere Pro

Change the lighting of any clip — from overcast grey to golden sunset, from daylight to moody neon — without leaving your timeline. FXbuddy sends your clip to Runway Gen-4 and returns a relit version in under 90 seconds.

How it works

  1. Select the clip on your timeline you want to relight.
  2. Open FXbuddy and type your lighting description in plain English.
  3. FXbuddy sends the clip to Runway Gen-4 Turbo and drops the result back onto your timeline.

Example prompts

  • Relight this scene with a warm golden-hour sunset. Soft orange fill from camera left.
  • Change the lighting to moody blue neon. Night exterior. Wet pavement reflections.
  • Make this look like a candlelit room. Warm, flickering, deep shadows in the background.
  • Overcast flat light. Remove any directional shadows.

Best for

  • Fixing continuity between angles shot at different times of day
  • Elevating run-and-gun footage for highlight reels
  • Narrative lighting shifts (flashbacks, time transitions)
  • Setting a lighting brief for your colourist to match
